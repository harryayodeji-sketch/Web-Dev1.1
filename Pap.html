How to Make Perfect Pancakes

Simple, fluffy and delicious pancakes you can make at home.

⏱ Cook Time
15 mins
Servings
4 people
⭐ Difficulty
Easy

Ingredients

Ingredient Quantity
Flour 1 cup
Sugar 1 tablespoon
Baking powder 1 teaspoon
Egg 1
Milk 3/4 cup
Melted butter 2 tablespoons

How to Make

  1. Mix the flour, sugar and baking powder.
  2. Add the egg and milk.
  3. Mix until everything is combined.
  4. Heat a pan and add a little butter.
  5. Pour some batter into the pan.
  6. Cook until golden brown on both sides.
  7. Serve and enjoy!
